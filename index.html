Who We Are

Spreading Smiles to Every Street Child

Come for Road Child (CRC), MBSTU is a voluntary student organization dedicated to uplifting underprivileged street children. Founded by enthusiastic students of Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University, CRC works relentlessly to provide basic education, nutrition, warm clothing, and healthcare support.

Every child deserves rights, compassion, and a fair opportunity to dream. Together, our volunteer community turns university spirit into life-changing humanitarian impact.

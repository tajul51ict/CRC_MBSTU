About Come for Road Child (CRC)

Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU)

The Organization

Who We Are

Come for Road Child (CRC), MBSTU is a compassionate, student-led socio-voluntary organization founded on the core belief that no child belongs on the cold street without care, education, and hope. Operating actively from the Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University campus in Santosh, Tangail, our members work with dedication to bridge the gap between privileged society and vulnerable street children.

We organize open-air education sessions, distribute nutritious food, provide seasonal warm clothing, run emergency medical camps, and celebrate festive occasions with children who lack parental or social support.

Key Organizational Values
  • Empathy & Humanity: Treating every underprivileged child with dignity, warmth, and respect.
  • Educational Empowerment: Guiding street children towards literacy and formal primary schooling.
  • Integrity & Transparency: Ensuring 100% of volunteer efforts and charitable donations directly benefit the children.
  • Student Leadership: Fostering civic responsibility and social consciousness among MBSTU students.

Our Mission

To transform the lives of street children by providing non-formal open-air education, basic healthcare, clean clothes, nutrition, and psychological care, while steering them towards safety, school enrollment, and self-reliance away from street risks and exploitation.

Our Vision

To build a society where no innocent child is left stranded on the streets of Tangail or Bangladesh, where every child enjoys their fundamental human rights: a safe shelter, nutritious meals, access to primary education, and a dignified future.

Our Initiatives

What We Do

Practical humanitarian actions carried out continuously by our student volunteer community.

Free Open Pathshala

Every Friday and Saturday afternoon, our volunteers conduct open-air learning classes covering Bengali, English alphabets, counting, and moral values.

Winter Warmth Drives

During cold winter seasons, we collect and distribute hundreds of high-quality blankets, sweaters, socks, and warm clothes to street kids sleeping at railway stations and bus terminals.

Health & Hygiene Camps

Regular free health checkups in partnership with student doctors and health clubs, accompanied by soap, toothbrush, and essential first aid distribution.

Why Volunteer with CRC MBSTU?

Volunteering with CRC MBSTU is not just an extracurricular activity; it is a transformative personal journey. As a volunteer, you will:

  • Experience the profound joy of seeing a vulnerable child smile and read their first word.
  • Develop real-world organizational, teamwork, leadership, and public speaking skills.
  • Build strong friendships and networks across all university departments and batches.
  • Receive recognition and volunteer certificates honoring your humanitarian contribution.
